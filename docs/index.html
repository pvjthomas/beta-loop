workflow · mastermix/workflows/tem1_activity_screen.json

Running the combined TEM-1 screen

One workflow file now does the whole job: it dilutes its own reagents — TEM-1 enzyme, 9 active test compounds plus a known-active control, matched vehicle, and (just-in-time) nitrocefin — then builds a 36-well assay plate, mixes it, pre-incubates, develops the color reaction in a stagger-aware order, and runs a real kinetic A490 read.

regenerated · v3 Two more upstream changes since the last version: (1) the plate moved to the R2 v5 column-strip layout — 36 wells instead of 39, no two occupied wells horizontally adjacent, and T1262 tazobactam is now Compound 1 (was Compound 2) since clavulanic acid is no longer double-counted as a test compound; (2) the single A490 read became a real kinetic schedule — 21 reads over 10 minutes with a defined slope-fit window, replacing 3 hand-scheduled endpoint reads.

02Phase 0

Preparing every working solution before assay setup

The very first node, prepare_dilutions, runs the prepare_tem1_dilution_plate skill before the workflow even picks up a pipette for the assay itself. It performs five distinct dilutions on wellplate_pcr_parts_4 (the working plate) and in cold-block tubes:

1
TEM-1 enzyme, 2-step dilution 100 ng/µL → 0.1 ng/µL
2 µL stock (hole_1) + 198 µL BLB (hole_6) → 200 µL at 1 ng/µL intermediate (hole_2), mixed; then 100 µL of that + 900 µL more BLB → 1000 µL at 0.1 ng/µL working solution (hole_8), mixed again. A real 100× then 10× dilution — 1000× total.
2
T1262 tazobactam (now Compound 1), special low-dose intermediate 10 mM → 200 µM
49 µL BLB (hole_5) + 1 µL of 10 mM T1262 stock — read from compound_1_source_plate/compound_1_source_well — → 50 µL at 200 µM in hole_3, mixed. Every other compound targets a 50 µM assay dose (per literature, most default to a project-wide HTS convention); T1262's is set from a 10× IC50 rule, landing at 1 µM, so it gets its own intermediate tube instead of the standard recipe.
3
Batch-dispense BLB into 10 working wells batched · 47.5 µL
Positive control + all 9 active compound wells (including T1262's) get their diluent from BLB tube 1 (hole_5) in one multi-dispense pass. The unused 10th slot has no dest well, so it's automatically filtered out of this batch.
4
Batch-dispense BLB into the vehicle well batched · 95 µL
From BLB tube 2 (hole_6) — a second physical tube exists purely to keep either tube under a ~1.5 mL practical fill limit, not for chemistry reasons.
5
Transfer stock/intermediate + DMSO into every well, then mix all 11 10× stock + 1× DMSO
9 compounds + positive control each get 2.5 µL from their own source well — except Compound 1 (T1262), which gets 2.5 µL from its 200 µM intermediate (hole_3) instead of raw stock. Vehicle gets 5 µL DMSO. All 11 wells are then mixed (5 cycles).

Result: positive control + 8 standard compounds at 500 µM, T1262 at 10 µM, vehicle at 5% DMSO, and TEM-1 at 0.1 ng/µL — all sitting ready before Phase I even starts.

04Phase II & III

Mix, then wait for compounds to meet TEM-1

Unchanged from prior versions. The plate takes an out-and-back trip to the shaker (13 discrete open/load/close/run/wait/unload/return skill calls, since the shaker can't be trusted to hold or release the plate on its own), then sits through a 10-minute room-temperature pre-incubation before anything reacts with nitrocefin.

place pipette→ grab plate→ open · load · close shaker→ run · on→ wait 1 min→ run · off→ open · unload · close shaker→ return plate · grab pipette→ pre-incubate 10 min
05Phase IV

Nitrocefin, prepared just-in-time and dosed in a staggered order

New skill: prepare_nitrocefin_working_solution runs right after pre-incubation ends — not back in Phase 0 — because the working solution is meant to go onto the plate immediately after it's made.

1
Dilute nitrocefin, 20 mM → 100 µM just-in-time
1243.75 µL BLB (hole_7, chunked across pipette-volume-limited transfers) + 6.25 µL of 20 mM stock (hole_4) → 1250 µL at 100 µM in hole_10, then mixed. This is the "2×" nitrocefin: still 2-fold above its 50 µM in-well target, because 25 µL lands in a 50 µL well.
2
Dose 13 conditions in stagger-aware order 13× batched · 25 µL
Order: no-TEM-1 negative → positive control → compounds 1–9 (slot 10 skips, empty) → vehicle + TEM-1 last. The vehicle wells have no inhibitor at all, so they're the fastest-reacting condition on the plate — dosing them last minimizes the uncontrolled reaction time they accumulate before the first read.

08Phase V

A real kinetic read, not a snapshot

Place pipette → grab plate → open → load → close reader → one skill call, platereader_run_kinetic_schedule, which equilibrates at 25°C for 2 min, then reads A490 every 30 s for 10 min (21 reads total) → open → unload → close → return plate → save run.

This replaced the earlier version's 3 hand-scheduled reads (initial + 2 endpoints) with a proper time-series: 21 evenly spaced points is enough to fit a reaction-rate slope, not just compare two snapshots.

Kinetic schedule parameters

  • Equilibration: 120 s at 25°C after the lid closes, before the first read
  • Reads: every 30 s from t=0 to t=600 s (21 points) — data/screens/2/v5/kinetic_schedule.json lists each one with its own read_label (initial, t030s, t060s, …)
  • Slope-fit window: 180–480 s — the region of the curve actually used to compute each condition's reaction rate, avoiding the equilibration tail and any late plateau
  • Total time in reader: 120 + 600 = 720 s (12 min)

Every nitrocefin dispense node (Phase IV) now also carries a timing_label (positive_control, compound_1, …) — batched_dispense_mastermix records the actual wall-clock moment nitrocefin hits each condition. Since dosing is staggered across ~13 batches, not simultaneous, this timestamp is what lets analysis line up each condition's own reaction start against the one shared plate-reader clock.

10Changelog

What changed since the last walkthrough

  • One workflow, not two: the dilution-plate workflow is now the first node (prepare_dilutions) inside this file, instead of a separate tem1_dilution_plate.json that had to be run first by hand.
  • The stock/working-plate mismatch is fixed: compound, positive-control, and vehicle dispenses now source from the diluted working plate — previously they pointed at the same raw 10 mM stock wells the dilution plate itself used, a 20× overshoot.
  • TEM-1 is now really diluted: a genuine 2-step 100 ng/µL → 0.1 ng/µL dilution runs in Phase 0, instead of assuming hole_8 arrives pre-loaded.
  • Nitrocefin is now really diluted, and just-in-time: a dedicated prepare_nitrocefin_working_solution skill dilutes 20 mM stock to 100 µM immediately before dosing, rather than assuming hole_10 arrives pre-loaded.
  • New low-dose branch: T1262 tazobactam gets its own 200 µM intermediate tube to hit a 1 µM assay target, instead of the standard 50 µM every other compound uses.
  • Nitrocefin dosing is stagger-aware: negative → positive → 10 compounds → vehicle last, so the fastest-reacting condition accumulates the least uncontrolled time before the first read.
  • Kinetic read: 3 A490 timepoints (initial, +5 min, +10 min) instead of a single read.
  • R2 v5 relayout ("change blb hole"): clavulanic acid is no longer double-counted as both the positive control and a test compound, which bumped every compound down one slot — T1262 tazobactam moved from Compound 2 to Compound 1. The roster dropped to 9 active compounds; slot 10 is now a defined-but-empty placeholder (its dispense and nitrocefin nodes still exist in the graph but skip at runtime). The plate itself moved from a 39-well row-triplet "spaced interior" layout to a 36-well column-strip layout with no horizontally adjacent occupied wells.
  • Real kinetic read ("nitrocefin kinetic read schedule"): the 3 hand-scheduled A490 reads were replaced by platereader_run_kinetic_schedule — 120 s equilibration at 25°C, then 21 reads at 30 s intervals over 10 min, with a defined 180–480 s slope-fit window. Every nitrocefin dispense node also gained a timing_label so each condition's actual dosing time is recorded against the shared reader clock.
